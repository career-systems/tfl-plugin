Welcome, [Name]. This folder is your client engine. Your profile lives in `about/`, and everything you build lands in `outputs/`. It's all yours, on your machine.

## The one rule

**Always open Claude in this folder.** That's how it finds your profile and saves your work in the right place. If Claude ever seems to have forgotten who you are, you're probably in the wrong folder.

## The weekly routine

Monday, one sentence: **"Build my target list for this week."**
Claude runs your playbook's search instructions, you approve the companies worth pursuing, and it finds the right person at each.

Then, per company, one sentence: **"Build a Snapshot for [company]."**
Claude researches, writes, and formats. It stops 3 times to show you its work:

1. **The brief.** A one-page read on the company. You confirm the facts, especially the people.
2. **The page.** You read the whole page and say "send it," or "hold" with the fact that's wrong. Nothing on the page is edited by hand; a wrong fact is fixed in the brief and the page runs again.
3. **The send.** You upload the file, fill in the blanks that are yours, and hit send. Claude never sends anything.

## What lives where

- `about/` — your offer page, your messages, your search instructions, your voice. Say "my offer changed" anytime it does.
- `outputs/company-lists/` — your weekly shortlists.
- `outputs/company-briefs/` — one brief per company. Open it again before the call.
- `outputs/value-adds/` — the finished Snapshots you send.
- `outputs/messages/` — the messages that carry them.

## When something looks off

Say so in plain English. "That person left last year" or "this reads too formal" both work. Claude fixes it. If it keeps happening, tell Katie in the community so the skill itself gets fixed for everyone.
